Studio

Two Different Ways Of Seeing The Same Space.

We met while studying in Bergen. A year later, somehow, we ended up sharing a room. Living that close made one thing pretty obvious: we really liked making films together.

Emilio had spent the year before studying architectural visualisation. Oscar had spent his getting unusually good at flying FPV drones.

Bringing those two worlds together gave us a different way of filming spaces, combining an understanding of architecture with camera movement that would normally be impossible.

That became Levitere.
